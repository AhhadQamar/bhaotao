بھاؤ تاؤ

Bhaao Taao

Four stalls, one purse. Every shopkeeper has a hidden lowest price, and the list changes every game. Find it before he runs out of chai. Save enough and Atiq can stop at the bakery for cake.

Eyebrow up: you're far off. Slow sip of chai: getting warm. Glance at the next stall: nearly there. Read his face: one honest look, costs him a sip of patience.

Some of them bluff. Walking away is a bluff too.
